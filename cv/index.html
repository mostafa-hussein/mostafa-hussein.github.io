Professional Summary

Robot learning researcher with a PhD in robotics and 10+ years across research and production. I build visuomotor policies for long-horizon manipulation (imitation learning, DAgger, residual and sample-efficient RL, flow matching, and vision-language-action models) together with the data pipelines, evaluation protocols, and deployment loops that make them hold up on real robots. Shipped a production perception model to Amazon fulfillment centers, led multi-month in-home robot deployments in NIH-funded trials, and now lead continual policy improvement for humanoid manipulation at Persona AI. First author at ICRA, IROS, and CoRL.
