I build manipulation policies that work on real robots outside the lab. At Persona AI I lead policy learning for dual-arm and whole-body manipulation on humanoid robots: vision-language-action models, DAgger-style corrections, residual reinforcement learning, and continual improvement of policies after deployment.

Before that, I shipped the object-identification model behind Amazon's Sparrow picking robot, led multi-month in-home deployments of assistive robots at the University of New Hampshire, and co-founded [Raha Tech](https://rahatechllc.com) to turn that care robot into a product. PhD in robotics, UNH, 2023.
