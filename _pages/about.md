I'm a robot learning researcher. I build manipulation policies that work on real robots outside the lab. At Persona AI I work on policy learning for humanoid robots: vision-language-action (VLA) models, data corrections, and residual RL for dual-arm manipulation and whole-body control, with the goal of success rates that hold up in industrial settings.

Before Persona, I was an Applied Scientist at Amazon Robotics, where I built the multimodal object-identification model used by the Sparrow picking robot across U.S. fulfillment centers ([ICRA 2024](/publications/)). I then returned to the University of New Hampshire's Cognitive Assistive Robotics Lab (CARL) as an Applied Scientist. There I co-advised work on trajectory-consistent flow-matching policies (IROS 2026) and led in-home deployments of assistive robots in NIH-funded trials (ICSR 2025). I'm also a co-founder of [Raha Tech](https://rahatechllc.com), which is turning that care robot into a product for people living with dementia and their caregivers (patent pending).

I did my PhD at UNH with Prof. Momotaz Begum on robust behavior cloning: teaching robots multi-step tasks from imperfect human demonstrations (CoRL 2022, IROS 2021, ICRA 2019). Before that, I earned my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at Assiut University in Egypt, where I taught a NAO robot to write in English and Arabic from demonstrations.

**Interests:** Imitation Learning · VLA and generative policies (flow matching, diffusion) · continual and reinforcement learning · robot perception.
