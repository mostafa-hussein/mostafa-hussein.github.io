<p class="project-meta"><strong><a href="https://rahatechllc.com">Raha Tech</a></strong> · 2025 – present · Co-founder, technical engineering</p>

Raha Tech builds an AI care platform that pairs an autonomous mobile robot with smart-home sensors. It helps people living with dementia stay in their own homes and gives their family caregivers some relief. The robot runs daily care routines such as medication reminders, guided exercise, a companion voice, and overnight check-ins, and it follows up instead of only reminding.

## Where it comes from

The platform grew out of NIH-funded research at the University of New Hampshire (National Institute on Aging R01AG075892; NIBIB R21EB036448), where I led the robots' development and their deployment in private homes for one to six months at a time. The autonomy stack is described in the [CaRE-BT project](/projects/4_care_bt/). The system is deployed in real homes today, and a clinical trial comes next.

## My role

I co-founded the company and lead its technical engineering: the robot's autonomy, perception, and the path from a research prototype to a product that runs unattended in someone's home.
