**[Raha Tech](https://rahatechllc.com)** · Co-founder, Technical Engineering

Raha Tech builds an AI-enabled care platform that pairs an autonomous robot with smart-home sensors. It helps people living with dementia stay in their own homes and gives their family caregivers some relief. The robot handles daily care routines, such as reminders, guided exercise, a companion voice, and overnight check-ins, and it follows up instead of only reminding.

The platform grew out of our NIH-funded research at the University of New Hampshire (National Institute on Aging R01AG075892; NIBIB R21EB036448). It is already deployed in real homes, and a clinical trial comes next. The autonomy stack behind it is described in the [CaRE-BT project](/projects/4_care_bt/).
